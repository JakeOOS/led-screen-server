Delete your VOXEL account

You can delete your VOXEL account and its data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

From the app

  1. Open VOXEL and sign in.
  2. Open the Account section.
  3. Tap Delete account and type DELETE to confirm.

By email

If you cannot sign in, email jack@residentarchitects.com from the address on the account, asking for deletion. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your login — email address and password hash — is permanently removed.
  • Your saved screens, catalogues, schedule, messages, and news interests are permanently removed.
  • Any screens paired to your account are unpaired and reset, so the hardware can be set up again from scratch. The physical device is not affected.

No data is retained after deletion beyond what the law requires. See the privacy policy for the full detail.
