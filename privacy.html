VOXEL — Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 August 2026

VOXEL is an app for controlling a personal LED matrix screen. This policy explains what it stores, why, and how to get rid of it. It covers the Android app and the backend it talks to.

The app shows no adverts, contains no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and none of your data is sold or shared for advertising.

What is collected

DataWhy
Email address and password To create and sign in to your account. Authentication is handled by Supabase; your password is stored only as a salted hash, and we never receive or store it in readable form.
Screen configuration The screens, catalogues and schedule you build — including any message text you choose to display, your selected weather location, train station, and news interests. This is the content of the product; without it the screen has nothing to show.
Device pairing information An identifier for each screen you own, the name you give it, and the time it last contacted the server, so the app can show whether it is online.

No location is collected from your phone. The weather location is a place you type in, not a device GPS reading. The app requests no runtime permissions beyond internet access, and does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Who it is shared with

Data is passed to these providers only to make the product work:

ProviderWhat reaches them
SupabaseYour email address, password hash, and screen configuration. Acts as the authentication and database provider.
RenderHosts the backend; processes requests in transit.
OpenWeatherThe weather location you configured. No account identifier is sent.
Rail Data MarketplaceThe station you configured. No account identifier is sent.
AnthropicIf you enable the news screen, your news interests text and public RSS headlines are sent to the Claude API to pick and summarise a story. No account identifier is sent, and the data is not used to train models.

Beyond these, data is disclosed only where the law requires it.

Security

  • All traffic between the app, the backend, and the providers above uses HTTPS.
  • Every user-facing endpoint requires a valid authentication token, and the server checks that a screen belongs to you before returning or changing anything about it.
  • The app disables Android cloud backup, so your signed-in session cannot be copied to another device through a device backup or transfer.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your login and your saved configuration, and unpairs any screens on your account so they can be set up again by someone else. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot access the app, email jack@residentarchitects.com from the address on the account and ask for deletion. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

Children

VOXEL is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them.
